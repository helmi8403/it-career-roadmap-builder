Suggested resource stack by budget

Free core: official Microsoft Learn + Architecture Center + Well-Architected Framework + free Microsoft practice assessment + John Savill study playlist. Optional paid practice: choose one of Tutorials Dojo, MeasureUp or Whizlabs only after checking the content refresh date against April 2026. Optional structured subscription: Pluralsight if you need a sequenced curriculum rather than only practice questions. Do not buy all vendors; varied high-quality questions are more useful than repeated dumps.

Final checklist

  • I can map all four official domains to architecture decisions.

  • I can distinguish Azure RBAC, Entra roles, PIM, access reviews and Conditional Access.

  • I can draw DNS and traffic paths through private endpoints and hybrid networks.

  • I can choose SQL tiers, storage redundancy and migration methods based on limits.

  • I can justify AKS vs Container Apps vs App Service vs Functions.

  • I can distinguish queues, events, streams, backups, replication and failover groups.

  • I can explain RTO, RPO, recovery testing and business continuity assumptions.

  • I have evaluated an enterprise architecture case and documented design trade-offs.

  • I excluded unverified or missing-source questions from self-scoring.

  • I completed two fresh timed mocks and addressed weak official domains.

Source transparency: Exam weights, certification requirements and framework terminology were verified against official Microsoft sites on 24 October 2026. Non-Microsoft providers were reviewed for learning format and supplementary use; their pages vary in age and do not supersede the current official skills outline. The question-bank review status is based on the user-provided AZ-305 files, not Microsoft grading.
